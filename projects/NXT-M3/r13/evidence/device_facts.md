# Primary facts for engineering review

- UCC3803, TI SLUS270H electrical characteristics. UVLO startup min/typ/max 3.7/4.1/4.5 V, stop 3.2/3.6/4.0 V. At 5 V VCC/50 mA source, worst high-output drop 0.9 V. https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc3803.pdf
- A mini reference FDS2672 is a 200 V MOSFET, rated RDS(on) at 6 and 10 V, max threshold 4 V at 250 µA. The A R13 test switch is a proxy. https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/fds2672-d.pdf
- LM3488 Rev P tables 5.3 and 5.5. VIN 2.97–40 V, 100 kHz–1 MHz, FB 1.26 V typical and 1.24 to 1.28 V full range. Source driver typ 16 Ω at 5 V, max typical output swing Vin when Vin below 7.2 V. Sense 125–190 mV full range at 5 V. Compensation ramp 52–132 mV. The linked upstream ASC specifies 1.22 V in its divider calculation. TI model header Final 1.10 sets its internal reference to 1.26 V. https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lm3488.pdf
- B physical upstream README table at 4.61 V reports 179.5 V, 19.2 mA and 63.9% with resistive load. At 5.07 V it reports 180.0 V, 9.5 mA and 61.43%. The original board used a custom 10:145 EFD15 transformer. The ASC uses 10 µH primary, nominal K=.95, 33 mΩ shunt, 47 kΩ frequency resistor, 0.47 µF marked reservoir, IRFH7185 and SMDJ70CA model. The board BOM actually calls for SMBJ70CA and E1G. https://github.com/esynr3z/nixie-ps/tree/9df409c9770b6c32111396fc9dea318ff907d41d
- LM3488 TI model uses .SUBCKT LM3488_TRANS AGND COMP DR FA_SD FB ISEN PGND VIN. Its oscillator has a sin(2*3.142*V(N16819994)*TIME) source dependent on the programmed frequency node. Do not infer model speed, oscillation or silicon from syntax alone.
- IRFH7185 datasheet static RDS(on) guarantee applies to VGS=10 V, max VDS=100 V. A simulation pulse at low gate voltage cannot establish hot qualified on-resistance. https://www.infineon.com/part/IRFH7185
- C ADI LT3757(A) 2.9–40 V range. Coilcraft part selected by the upstream is a nominal 10 µH capacitor-charge magnetic coupled 1:10. No transformer isolation safety claim in the NXT common-ground circuit. https://www.analog.com/en/products/lt3757.html
- D current TI LM5155 specified frequency range 100 kHz to 2.2 MHz. The 80 kHz R11 point is a diagnostic generator, and hardware use below the nominal recommended floor needs a different design or supporting vendor evidence. https://www.ti.com/product/LM5155
