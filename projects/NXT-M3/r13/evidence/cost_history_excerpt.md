# History from R10 and R12

R10 BOM totals for 1, 3 and 10 modules are ¥247.38, ¥218.17 and ¥175.67 per module. These estimates include board and protection allowances, exclude assembly, freight and tubes, and predate the R11 Type-C work. They are neither present purchasing quotes nor a full updated R11 benchmark.

R12 frozen three-module subset consists of ESP32-S3 ¥25.50, four HC595s ¥17.76, OLED ¥9.78, sockets ¥8.40, PCB ¥13.00, sum ¥74.44. User-supplied tubes were historically excluded. An all-material cost should expose them as a separate unknown line. R11 USB-C input, more protection passives, assembly, hardware and new module electronics need a new source.
