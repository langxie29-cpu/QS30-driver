## Key engineering points a reviewer should check

**Both builders are build-only.** They create case trees, copy vendor models byte-for-byte (re-hashed against the R1 preregistration, mismatches abort), and explicitly *do not* pre-write `preregister.json` — `lab.execute()` must write that itself so the hash commit is real.

**Three traps I handled deliberately:**
- `LPOWER IC=(1.4,0,0,0,0)` has 5 spans. LTspice takes only the *first* value of an `IC=` list, so this sets exactly the 1.4 A mesh current; the 4-span form would instead initialise `V(in)` and the LPOWER current.
- The switch node (`drain`) and `b1`/`b2` internals are left **uninitialised on purpose** — no approved value exists, and writing 0 would make the cell quietly wrong (in the recovery regime `V(drain) > 89.17 V`).
- `V(fbmid)=45.017 V` and `V(fb)=0.83948 V` are both initialised, because an L1/latch failure there would silently feed a wrong voltage into the controller's feedback input.

**Two things I flagged rather than smoothed over:**
- The decision's quoted ramp-lag pair (12.58 mA → 18.27 mA) is inconsistent with a pure 10.34× τ scaling — roughly 130 mA would follow. `build_filter.py` back-checks this and warns; I did not quietly accept the number, and the principal should re-derive it.
- `run_passive_bounds.py` in the tree applies a `VEN en 0 0` SHDN hold that this decision does **not** authorise. I did not copy that policy.
